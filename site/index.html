Four jobs, done for you every day.

It wakes up at 7:00, does the busywork, and leaves you a short list of things only you can do.

Find

Reads Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, iCIMS, UKG, Oracle Cloud, Phenom, SuccessFactors, Jobvite, JazzHR and Workable boards directly, plus public internship lists. Any company it meets once gets watched from then on.

Rank

Every posting is read in full and scored 1 to 5 against your resume, with the reason in one line. Graduate-only roles, the wrong class year and seasons already past are hidden before you see them.

Fill the forms other autofills give up on

It clicks through to the real form, opens every dropdown to read the hidden options, types into search-as-you-type fields, attaches your resume, then checks each answer stuck and refills anything the page wiped. Anything your resume doesn't back is left for you, never guessed. Hits a sign-in wall (Workday)? It waits while you sign in, then keeps going.

Your answers

Answer a question once and it's reused when the same question, word for word, comes back. On a dropdown it's used only if your answer is one of the choices. Essays come back as a draft for you to edit, never filled. SSN, birth date, bank and login answers are never saved.

Track, from your inbox

Confirmation, assessment, interview and rejection emails update each application on their own. Quiet for two weeks? It reminds you to follow up.

Warm leads first

Drop in your LinkedIn connections and roles where you know someone get flagged, with a short note drafted from your real experience, ready to copy.

Three rules it never breaks.

Automation should take the busywork, not your judgment.

It never clicks Submit

Every application ends with you reading it and pressing the button yourself.

It never invents

Every answer points at your profile, a line in your resume, or an answer you gave yourself. No backing, no answer.

It never pretends to be you

No CAPTCHA solving, no LinkedIn automation, no sending messages. Drafts only.

Set it up in about ten minutes.

You need a Mac or Linux laptop, Chrome, and uv. Claude Code is optional for assisted setup.

Get the engine

Clone the repo, install it, and copy the example profile.

git clone https://github.com/omar-colab/internship-engine
cd internship-engine
uv sync
mkdir -p data
cp profile.example.json data/profile.json

Tell it about you

Edit data/profile.json with your information. Or use Claude Code's optional /setup flow to build the profile from your resume and run your first search.

Add the extension

Download the latest GitHub Release, extract it, and load the folder containing manifest.json from chrome://extensions with Developer mode on. Paste your token from engine token.
